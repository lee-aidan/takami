Our Mission

At Takami Engineering Group, we believe every successful project begins with trust.

Our mission is to guide homeowners, businesses, and developers through every stage of the design and construction process with integrity, technical excellence, and personal commitment. We believe our responsibility extends far beyond preparing plans. We become trusted partners, helping our clients navigate complex building codes, coordinate engineering disciplines, obtain permits, and bring their vision to life with confidence.

We approach every project with the understanding that behind every set of drawings is a family, a business, or an individual investing in their future. Whether it's a custom home, an addition, an ADU, a structural retrofit, or the restoration of an existing property, we are committed to delivering thoughtful design solutions while simplifying what can often be an overwhelming process.

Our work is guided by professionalism, accountability, and a genuine desire to serve others. We strive to build lasting relationships founded on honesty, communication, and follow-through, earning our clients' confidence one project at a time.

We don't simply prepare plans. We design solutions. We navigate the permitting process. We coordinate the professionals. We manage the details. We help build futures.

Our Philosophy

Great design is about more than beautiful buildings.

It is about listening carefully, solving problems thoughtfully, and serving people with excellence.

Every project presents unique challenges. Regulations change, permitting can be complicated, and construction requires countless decisions. Our role is to simplify that journey by providing experienced guidance from concept through completion.

We believe that integrity is just as important as technical expertise. Every recommendation we make, every drawing we prepare, and every decision we help our clients navigate is guided by a commitment to doing what is right.

Our goal is not simply to obtain permits. Our goal is to earn trust that lasts long after construction is complete.

A few words from our founder

Takami Engineering Group was founded on a simple belief: people deserve an advocate throughout the design and construction process.

Our clients place tremendous trust in us. They invite us into their homes, their businesses, and often some of the most important investments of their lives. We never take that responsibility lightly.

Success is not measured only by completed drawings or issued permits. It is measured by the relationships we build, the confidence we inspire, and the lasting value we create for those we serve.

LeAndre Davis · Founder
